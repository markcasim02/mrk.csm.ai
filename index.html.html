02

Inside the build

Real screenshots from the live RapidFlow system — the assistant config, the automations, the CRM, and the actual confirmation emails a test booking produced.// phone numbers, home addresses, and webhook URLs are redacted — the system is real, the personal data isn't public.

What it does: Vapi hosts the actual phone conversation. It runs the system prompt, wires together the transcriber (Deepgram), the reasoning model (GPT-4.1), and the voice (ElevenLabs), and exposes two custom tools the AI can call mid-call: book_appointment and log_call. Every call — booked or not — is recorded with a full transcript, so I can verify what actually happened instead of trusting what the AI says happened.

What it does: Make.com is the glue between the voice agent and the business's real systems. When the AI calls book_appointment, a webhook lands in Make, which checks Google Calendar for a genuinely open slot, creates the event, and sends two confirmation emails — one internal, one to the customer. A second, independent scenario logs every call outcome to HubSpot, whether or not it ended in a booking.

What it does: Every call — booked, transferred, or just an inquiry — lands in HubSpot as a contact and a deal, moved into the right pipeline stage automatically by a switch()-based routing formula. This gives the business full visibility into call outcomes, not just the successful bookings.

What it does: Once Make confirms a slot is free, Google Calendar gets the real event, and two emails go out automatically — an internal alert to the business owner and a confirmation to the customer — closing the loop without a human touching any of it.
